📰 Article Clipper

Extract article data for Google Sheets with one click

📋 Article Clipper

⬆️ Drag this button to your bookmarks bar

How to Install

  1. Show your bookmarks bar (Chrome: Cmd+Shift+B / Ctrl+Shift+B)
  2. Drag the purple button above directly to your bookmarks bar
  3. Done! The bookmarklet is ready to use

How to Use

  1. Navigate to any news article
  2. Click the "Article Clipper" bookmark
  3. A green notification confirms the data is copied
  4. Switch to Google Sheets and press Cmd+V / Ctrl+V
  5. Data appears in 5 columns automatically

Output Format

Data is copied as tab-separated values for direct paste into Google Sheets:

Column A Column B Column C Column D Column E
URL Headline Date (YYYY-MM-DD) Author/Byline Article Text
Note: Article text is limited to 5,000 characters. The extraction uses the same logic as the MizzouNewsCrawler pipeline (JSON-LD, OpenGraph, meta tags, CSS selectors).
