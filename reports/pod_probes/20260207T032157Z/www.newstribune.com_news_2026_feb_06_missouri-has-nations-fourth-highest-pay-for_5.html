Missouri has nation’s fourth-highest pay for carpenters

17 hours, 23 minutes ago by Natalie-Elizabeth Tan
  0 Comments

Missouri ranks fourth in the nation for carpenter pay, although Jefferson City pays the least among the cities surveyed, according to a report published by Construction Coverage.
